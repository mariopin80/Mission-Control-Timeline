MISSION CONTROL TIMELINE — PRIVACY NOTICE
Version 1.0 — 2026

Mission Control Timeline does not require an account and does not include
advertising, analytics, tracking or telemetry operated by the author.

The application stores station settings, selected NORAD numbers, preferences
and an orbital-data cache locally on the user’s computer. These files remain
under the user’s Windows profile and are not sent to the author.

To update orbital data, the application makes internet requests to independent
providers such as CelesTrak and SatNOGS. Those providers receive technical
connection data normally present in an internet request, including the public
IP address, date, time and requested catalog number. Their privacy policies and
server logs are outside the author’s control.

The application does not intentionally transmit station coordinates, station
name or sound/language preferences to the author. Uninstalling the application
may leave user settings in the local application-data folder so they can be
reused after reinstalling; the user may delete that folder manually.

Questions: contact the author through the official project distribution channel.
